# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally:

- **Empresas y tutores de prácticas/empleo** que evalúan a un estudiante de FP DAM y deciden si contactarle.
- **Pequeños negocios** que podrían encargar una web, como ya hicieron SERVITEK E.A.S. y Solca Decoraciones.

Both arrive from a link (GitHub, Instagram, CV, WhatsApp) and decide in under a minute whether to keep reading and get in touch.

## Product Purpose

Web personal de Iván Vera: presenta quién es, qué está aprendiendo, qué ha construido y cómo contactarle. Success = the visitor reaches a project or a contact channel convinced that Iván takes development seriously.

## Positioning

Estudiante de primer curso que aprende en público: los ejercicios de Java son reales y básicos, y convive con dos webs reales publicadas para clientes de Paraguay. El trabajo con IA (Claude, ChatGPT, opencode) es parte declarada de su forma de aprender. Honest junior, not a pretend senior.

## Operating Context

Static one-page site on GitHub Pages (`ivanvera7.github.io/Ivanvera-web`), plain HTML/CSS/JS with no build step. The only third-party script is the official Instagram embed, loaded on demand.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Six sections in this order: Inicio, Sobre mí, Stack, Proyectos, Instagram, Contacto.
- Content is in Spanish.
- No dependencies or build tooling unless strictly necessary.
- Must work on phones (iOS/Android), tablets, laptops and large monitors.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Iván Vera (displayed as "Ivan Vera" in the hero).
- Dark background is binding. Everything else visual (accent color, terminal prompt signature, typography) may be replaced.
- Personal photo: `assets/avatar.jpg`. Tool logos: `assets/logos/`. App icon: `favicon/`.

## Evidence on Hand

- 5 Java exercise repos (Hola Mundo, Variables, Sumar, Calculadora, Info de usuario) with real code snippets.
- 2 live client websites: servitek.pages.dev (Next.js 14, TypeScript, Tailwind, GitHub Actions, Cloudflare Pages) and solca-decoraciones.vercel.app.
- 4 Instagram posts from @ivanvera7_.
- Facts: 27 años, paraguayo, vive en Madrid, FP DAM en Ilerna, idiomas español · guaraní · inglés · portugués.
- Absent and must not be fabricated: work experience, clients beyond the two above, testimonials, certifications, metrics.

## Product Principles

1. Honesty over impressiveness: show real work at its real level.
2. The two live websites are the strongest proof; make them easy to open.
3. Contact must be one tap away from anywhere.
4. Craft is the argument: the site itself demonstrates care.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus, `prefers-reduced-motion`.
